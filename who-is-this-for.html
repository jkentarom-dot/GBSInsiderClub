Who is this for?

Two minutes to find out whether this site is built for you. You will see the roles and levels we write for, and how far a GBS career can go from here.

Who It's For

Built for GBS professionals at every stage.

Whether you joined last month or lead a team of fifty — if you work in Global Business Services, Shared Services, or a Center of Excellence, this is for you.

Content mapped to your career stage
Early Career — Associates and Senior Associates
  • First job in GBS — understand what you joined and how it works
  • Struggling with expectations — what actually gets you promoted
  • Want to earn more but don't know where to start
  • Looking to move from current role or organization
  • English is not your first language — all content is clear and accessible
Mid Career — Team Leads and Managers
  • Ready to move from doing to leading — need the frameworks
  • Building credibility with senior stakeholders for the first time
  • Want to understand the commercial logic behind the work you deliver
  • Looking for structured development your organization doesn't provide
  • Preparing for Director or Senior Manager roles
Leaders — Directors and Above
  • Looking for structured content to develop your team
  • Want an external reference to benchmark against best practice
  • Building a GBS or transitioning one — need orientation material
  • Seeking advisory perspective on operating model decisions

Every role below is held by someone who started where you are now.
