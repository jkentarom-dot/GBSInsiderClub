Pillar 7 of 10

Leadership & People Management

From first-time manager to strategic leader. Team operations, performance, and org design.

The transition from individual contributor to manager is one of the hardest in any career — and in GBS it happens fast. The skills that made you effective as an analyst or specialist are not the same ones that make you effective as a leader. This pillar covers the full arc: first-time management through to strategic leadership.

Why this pillar matters

You got promoted because you were good at your job. Now the job has changed. This pillar covers what nobody teaches — from the first awkward one-on-one with your former peer, through building a team rhythm, to reading a P&L and making org design decisions. If you manage people or plan to, start here.

Who needs this

New managers, team leads stepping up, senior professionals preparing for their first leadership role.
