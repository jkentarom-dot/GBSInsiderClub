GBS Transition (Migration)

Transition strategy, knowledge transfer execution, and stabilisation.

Transitioning processes into a GBS centre is one of the highest-risk activities in shared services. Done well, it sets up a function for years of stable delivery. Done poorly, it creates years of remediation work. This pillar covers the full lifecycle — from strategy and scoping through knowledge transfer to hypercare and stabilisation.
