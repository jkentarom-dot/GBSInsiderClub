Why this pillar matters

This is ground zero. If you just joined a GBS center, transferred from the business, or want to understand what GBS actually is beyond the org chart — start here. These guides cover operating models, value creation, governance, contracts, performance metrics, and location strategy. You will leave understanding not just what GBS does, but why it exists and how it makes money.

Who needs this

New GBS joiners, business transfers into shared services, anyone who needs the full picture before their first stakeholder meeting.
