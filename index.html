Questions

Before you start.

Is this only for finance?

No. It covers GBS, Shared Services, BPO, consulting and transformation roles across functions — finance, HR, IT, procurement, master data, and customer service.

Is this useful if I am not a manager?

Yes. Most tools are designed for associates, specialists, SMEs and future team leads. The leadership material sits further along the path.

Is this useful if I work in BPO?

Yes. The career mechanics are the same: KPIs, visibility, stakeholder trust, service quality, automation and progression.

Is this useful if English is not my first language?

Yes. The site uses plain workplace language and templates you can adapt directly.

Do I need to read everything?

No. Start with your role or the problem in front of you. Come back when your situation changes.

What makes this different from generic career advice?

It is built around the real mechanics of GBS work: KPIs, transitions, process ownership, service delivery, stakeholder management, automation and internal mobility.
